## 3. Booking Alerts — No Obligation to Accept / Alertas de Reserva — Sin Obligación de Aceptar

number when Clients request transportation in Driver/Subscriber's area.

3.2 **Driver/Subscriber may freely accept or reject any booking alert without penalty,
deduction, suspension, or any adverse consequence** to their subscription or Platform
access. No minimum acceptance rate is required.

3.3 The Company makes **no guarantee** of the volume, frequency, or geographic
distribution of booking alerts.

## 4. Fares and Payments / Tarifas y Pagos

4.1 **Driver/Subscriber sets its own fares** or agrees on fares directly with Clients.
The Platform does not fix, mandate, or regulate ride fares.

4.2 **Ride fare payments are made directly between Client and Driver/Subscriber.**
The Company does not collect, hold, process, or receive ride fare payments.

4.3 **Driver/Subscriber pays the Company a fixed monthly software subscription fee**
as agreed at the time of plan selection. This is the only payment obligation to
the Company.

4.4 Subscription fees are non-refundable except as required by applicable law.
