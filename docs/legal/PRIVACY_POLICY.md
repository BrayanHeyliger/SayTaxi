## 2. Data We Collect / Datos que Recolectamos

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|----------|----------|---------|
| Identity | Name, email address, phone number | Account creation, communication |
| Location | Pickup/dropoff coordinates (Client-provided) | Booking alert generation |
| Technical | IP address, device type, browser, app version | Security, analytics |
| Usage | Pages visited, features used, session duration | Product improvement |
| Subscription | Billing name, last 4 digits of card (via Stripe) | Subscription management |

**We do NOT collect or process ride fare payment data.** Ride fares are settled
directly between Clients and Drivers. Our payment processor (Stripe) handles
subscription payments under its own privacy and security policies.

## 3. How We Use Your Data / Cómo Usamos sus Datos

We use collected data to:

- Transmit booking alerts/leads to subscribed Drivers;
- Authenticate users and manage accounts;
- Process Driver software subscription payments;
- Provide customer support;
- Comply with legal obligations;
- Improve Platform features and performance.

We do **NOT** sell personal data to third parties.

## 4. Data Sharing / Compartir Datos

We may share data with:

- **Drivers:** Client booking details (pickup/dropoff, name, phone) necessary to
  fulfill the booking alert. Only the minimum necessary information is shared.
- **Service Providers:** Stripe (payment processing), hosting providers, analytics
  tools — all under appropriate data processing agreements.
- **Law Enforcement:** When required by valid legal process.
