## Part A — SayTaxi Company Licensing (Software Business)

### Business Tax Receipt (BTR) Registration

SayTaxi must register its economic activity under the correct category to avoid being
classified as a Transportation Network Company (TNC) or Vehicle for Hire operator.

**Recommended NAICS/Business Categories:**

| Jurisdiction | Category to Use |
|---|---|
| City of Orlando | Software Development / Internet Services / Advertising |
| Orange County, FL | Software Development / Information Technology Services |
| State of Florida (Sunbiz) | Software Publisher / Internet Advertising Services |

**Categories to AVOID:**

- ❌ Transportation Network Company (TNC)
- ❌ Vehicle for Hire / Taxicab Service
- ❌ Transportation Broker
- ❌ Limousine Service

### Florida Registration Checklist (SayTaxi Company)

- [ ] Register entity with Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz): [sunbiz.org](https://www.sunbiz.org)
- [ ] Obtain City of Orlando Business Tax Receipt (BTR) under Software/IT category
- [ ] Obtain Orange County Local Business Tax Receipt under Software/IT category
- [ ] File for EIN with IRS (if not already obtained)
- [ ] Open dedicated business bank account separate from personal funds
- [ ] Confirm with a Florida attorney that NO TNC or Vehicle for Hire permits are required for a pure SaaS/Lead Generator model
- [ ] Consult with Florida attorney on sales tax obligations for SaaS subscriptions under F.S. § 212

## Part B — Driver/Taxi Company Compliance Checklist

Each Driver or taxi company using SayTaxi software is **independently responsible** for
maintaining all required licenses, insurance, and permits. SayTaxi does **not** provide
or guarantee compliance on behalf of Drivers.

### Florida Vehicle for Hire — Driver/Operator Checklist

#### State Level
- [ ] Valid Florida Driver's License (Class E minimum for non-commercial; commercial CDL if applicable)
- [ ] Clean driving record (check requirements with Orange County / City of Orlando)
- [ ] Vehicle registered in Florida with current title

#### Orange County / City of Orlando Level
- [ ] **Vehicle for Hire Permit** — issued by Orange County Comptroller or applicable municipality
  - Contact: Orange County Comptroller, Taxpayer Services Division
  - Website: [comptroller.orangecountyfl.net](https://comptroller.orangecountyfl.net)
- [ ] **Background check** clearance as required by local ordinance
- [ ] **Vehicle inspection** certificate from authorized inspection station
- [ ] **Business Tax Receipt (BTR)** for taxi/transportation operation in applicable jurisdiction

#### Insurance Requirements
- [ ] **Commercial Automobile Insurance** meeting Florida minimum requirements for Vehicle for Hire operations
  - Minimum limits vary; consult Orange County ordinances and Florida F.S. § 627.7415
- [ ] Proof of insurance must be carried in the vehicle at all times
- [ ] Policy must name the correct insured (individual driver or company)

#### Orlando International Airport (MCO) — if operating from airport
- [ ] **MCO Ground Transportation Permit** issued by the Greater Orlando Aviation Authority (GOAA)
  - Contact: GOAA Ground Transportation Office
  - Website: [orlandoairports.net](https://www.orlandoairports.net)
- [ ] Annual permit renewal
- [ ] Vehicle must meet GOAA vehicle age and condition requirements
- [ ] GOAA-mandated markings/decals on vehicle

#### Tax Compliance (Driver/Operator)
- [ ] Report all income from transportation services on federal and state tax returns
- [ ] Consider quarterly estimated tax payments (self-employment)
- [ ] Maintain mileage and expense records for deductions

## Part C — Recommended Ongoing Compliance Actions

### For SayTaxi (Software Company)
1. **Annual legal review** of Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Driver Agreement with Florida counsel.
2. **Monitor regulatory changes** in Florida and Orange County regarding software platforms and lead generators.
3. **Do NOT** add features that involve: (a) setting or collecting ride fares; (b) mandatory trip assignments; (c) driver performance metrics that simulate employment; (d) vehicle dispatch functions — as these could re-classify the platform as a TNC.

### For Drivers Using SayTaxi
1. Renew all permits annually before expiration.
2. Maintain insurance continuously — lapses may void coverage and violate Vehicle for Hire requirements.
3. Report any license suspensions or violations to SayTaxi immediately per the Driver Agreement.
